What They Share

Depth through layers

All three use layered transparency and subtle gradients — not flat colors. Art Cards: gold over black. Dreamspace: lavender over void. Proposal: earth tones over cream. The common language is luminosity emerging from darkness.

Warm + Cool tension

Art Cards: warm gold with purple undertones. Dreamspace: cool lavender with warm rose accent. Proposal: warm earth with cool grey undertones. The brand lives in the space between warm and cool — fitting for something called "imaginal."

Glow & shimmer

Art Cards: gold shimmer sweeps, sparkle particles. Dreamspace: lavender glow, floating orbs, pulse effects. Even the proposal page has a subtle drift animation. All three treat light as alive.

Serif + Sans pairing

Art Cards: Cinzel + Inter. Proposal: Cormorant Garamond + Inter. Dreamspace breaks this with Quicksand alone — but the pattern is clear: a classical serif for soul, modern sans for interface.
