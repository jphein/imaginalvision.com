Three Approaches for imaginalvision.com

Landing page structure — pick the one that feels right

A

Portal Hub — Equal Weight

A beautiful entry page with the Imaginal Vision brand at top, then three cards linking to each offering: Art Cards, Dreamspace, and Creative Sight Counseling. Minimal text — mostly visual. Like a gallery entrance with three doors.

Pros

  • Clean, simple, fast to build
  • Each project gets equal presence
  • Easy to add new offerings later

Cons

  • Doesn't tell the story — just a menu
  • Visitors need to click again to learn anything
  • Weaker for SEO (thin content)
B ✓

Narrative Scroll — Tools as Story (Selected)

A single-page scroll that tells the story: who Cassandra is → what the imaginal means → the tools she's built (Art Cards, Dreamspace) → who they're for (clients, students, fellow therapists) → Creative Sight Counseling practice info → contact. The domain proposal page's style, but as a real site.

Pros

  • Tells the full story — visitors understand the vision
  • Great for SEO (rich content, keywords)
  • Builds trust before asking people to try tools
  • Works for all 4 audiences

Cons

  • More content to write and maintain
  • Longer to build
  • Risk of being too long if not curated well
C

Hero + Cards — Quick Impact

A bold hero section with "Imaginal Vision — Expressive Art Therapy & Digital Tools" and Cassandra's tagline, followed immediately by two featured tool cards with "Try it" buttons. Below that, compact "About" section and link to Creative Sight Counseling.

Pros

  • Fast to grasp — tools are front and center
  • Gets people into the tools quickly
  • Balanced: not too thin, not too long

Cons

  • Less room for the philosophical depth
  • Counseling practice is secondary

Aesthetic DNA — Finding the Common Thread

Three sites, one visual language waiting to emerge

The Three Palettes

Art Cards

Black → Dark brown → Gold → Cream
Font: Cinzel (serif) + Inter

Ornate, mystical, tarot. Sacred geometry, gold shimmer, sparkle particles.

Dreamspace

Deep violet → Purple → Lavender → Moonlight → Rose
Font: Quicksand (sans-serif)

Cosmic, dreamy, creative. Floating orbs, glow effects, particle drift.

Creative Sight Counseling

Warm cream → Sand → Earth → Dark brown
Font: Cormorant Garamond (serif) + Inter

Warm, grounded, contemplative. Clean minimalism with depth.

What They Share

Depth through layers

All three use layered transparency and subtle gradients. The common language is luminosity emerging from darkness.

Warm + Cool tension

Gold ↔ Purple ↔ Earth. The brand lives in the space between warm and cool — fitting for "imaginal."

Glow & shimmer

All three treat light as alive — gold shimmer, lavender glow, drift animations.

Serif + Sans pairing

A classical serif for soul, modern sans for interface.
